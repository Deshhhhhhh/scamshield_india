Your OTPs and phone numbers are not our business

Every message passes through a scrubber before any AI sees it or anything is stored:

  • Phone numbers, OTPs, Aadhaar, PAN, card numbers, UPI IDs, emails → masked ([PHONE], [OTP]…)
  • Only the scrubbed text + a one-way hash are stored — a database leak exposes no PII
  • No raw messages are logged — ever. Not even for debugging
  • Open methodology: red-flag library and model are auditable in the repo

Honest answers

Is ScamShield a replacement for common sense?

No. It’s a decision-support tool. Even a “safe” verdict means “no known scam patterns”, not a guarantee. When in doubt, verify via official apps and helplines.

Will it work for Hindi messages?

Yes — explanations come in English or Hindi (Devanagari or romanized). The model is trained on Hinglish lure patterns like “aapka account band ho jayega”.

What does it cost?

Users pay nothing. The project runs on free tiers and runs locally with docker compose up — zero spend by design.

Can the AI be wrong?

Yes — false positives happen (we publish them in our model card). That’s why we explain why: you always see the red flags, not just a score.

How do I report a scam I saw?

Forward it to the bot, and report it at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. Both matter.
